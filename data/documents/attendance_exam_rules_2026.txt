ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2026 - ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
A minimum of 75% aggregate attendance across lectures, practical laboratory sessions, and tutorial hours is mandatory to be eligible for semester final examinations.
Students with attendance between 65% and 74% due to verified medical emergencies must file an appeal with medical documentation to the Dean of Academic Affairs within 7 working days.
Students falling below 65% attendance are automatically barred from end-term examinations and must repeat the course in summer term.
