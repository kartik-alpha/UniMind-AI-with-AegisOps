HOSTEL POLICY 2026 - CAMPUS RESIDENCE REGULATIONS
Mandatory hostel curfew is 10:00 PM for all undergraduate resident students.
Late entries require prior digital approval from the hostel warden submitted at least 2 hours in advance via the student portal.
Quiet hours begin strictly at 11:00 PM on weekdays and 12:00 AM on weekends.
External visitors are permitted in designated ground-floor visitor lounges between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM daily.
Possession of prohibited electrical heating appliances or hazardous materials is strictly forbidden.
